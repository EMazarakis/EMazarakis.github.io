# Introduction
Having sat on the interviewer’s side for 30+ interviews for Data Analyst, Data Engineer & Software Engineer roles, I noticed a common pattern—from Junior to Senior level: 
anxiety often causes candidates to forget key parts of their experience.

No one has ever presented their CV to me using a short slide deck!

A quick presentation on Teams/Zoom:
- Makes you stand out immediately.
- Gives you control of the conversation.
- Reduces anxiety – you won't forget a thing.
